05 / HOUSE WISDOM

Good to know

The tiny details that make the weekend run smoothly.

♧Food01
  • Handwash Freddie’s bowl and slow feeder before each meal.
  • Canned pumpkin is in the fridge or on the second shelf of the lower pantry cabinet.
  • Louis uses the same towel for the day. Extra dog towels are in the black cabinet with the dog supplies.
♨Water02

There are two water bowls:

  • In the office
  • In the dining area, at the end of the island by Freddie’s bowl
⇄Dog door03
  • Freddie is very autonomous and has outdoor access all the time.
  • The dog door can be covered at night, but make sure the cats are inside first—they use it too.
  • The door is generally left open in case a pet has an upset stomach.
  • Louis is afraid of the dog door.
✳Play04
  • Freddie will play Frisbee forever if you’re willing, but make sure she rests.
  • Louis likes to wrestle with Freddie. Keep the intensity down by interrupting with treats.
  • Freddie may resource-guard a toy or area. If she lashes out at Louis, pick him up, take a breath, and give him a squeeze as a reset.
⌁Potty & litter05
  • Louis hasn’t figured out the dog door yet. Take him outside every few hours, and after he wakes up, eats, or naps.
  • Freddie has outdoor access through the dog door. If covering it at night, make sure the cats are inside.
  • Scoop litter into the green bags next to the downstairs litter box in the boy cave. Take the trash out the side door.
  • Keep the dogs out of the boy cave; they may eat the litter or poop.
☾Sleeping06
  • Freddie may sleep under the bed, on a living-room chair, in the office, or eventually with the sitter.
  • The cats will likely pile on the sitter.
⌂Room gating07
  • The guest bedroom and bedroom doors are usually closed or gated during the day.
  • Freddie and Louis like to wrestle on the beds and play under them.
  • The cats need access to the boy cave. Keep the dogs out of that area.
✧Food on the counter08
  • Any pet may grab boxes, bags, or food, so keep them out of reach.
  • Freddie may snatch boxes, especially when they’re being broken down for recycling.
  • Freddie has also been stealing food wrappers recently.
☼Lights09

The house has smart lighting:

  • Living room: The Flic button is on the staircase switches. One press turns the lights on; a double press turns them off.
  • Guest bedroom: The Flic button is on the switch. One press toggles the lights; a double press turns them off. The globe-light switches can also be used.
  • Patio: The lights turn on at sundown and off at midnight.
  • Tea Room: Press the switch once to toggle the canned lights. Double-press the light switch to turn the lamps on; triple-press to turn them off.
  • Porch: Lights follow the same schedule as the patio lights.
❋Cleaning supplies10
  • Trash is between the sink cabinets and the three-stack of drawers.
  • Paper towels and general cleaning supplies are under the sink.
  • Disinfectant, Pine-Sol, bathroom cleaner, and similar supplies are in the hall closet (the door on the left when facing the bathroom) or under the bathroom sink.
  • The Dyson vacuum is under the guest bed; attachments are in the hall closet.
